Title: Short-Wall Policy + Multi-Hive Post-Mortem (Revised)
Version: 2025-09-07
Supersedes: Update_16.txt (archive as Update_16_preMH.txt)

0) Summary (what changed)
- Keep **Single-Hive (SH) Large-N** as default for short walls (≤30 min).
- **Disable MH-Full** at short walls; add **MH-Lite** (H1/H3 only) as *opt-in experiment*.
- **Late-only Or-opt** with portal protection; no early global relocations.
- Boundary discipline: small halos, **portals-only** crossings, tight per-node edge caps.
- Merge cadence: **POPMUSIC only at mid & end** (no per-epoch merges).

1) Defaults (short-wall runs: pr2392@600 s, fnl4461@1800 s)
- Preset: SH Large-N = Delaunay ∪ k-NN, k = k_base+4; POPMUSIC; 4 agents/zone; Or-opt OFF; workers=1; threads=1.
- Controller: **re-sum every tour via TSPLIB integer**; that integer drives champion, rewards, allocator Δgap/sec, bounds, TTT, logs.
- Parity triad once; abort if .opt.tour exists and parity ≠ OK.

2) MH-Lite (experimental; OFF by default)
Goal: retain some diversity without short-wall coordination churn.
- Hives: **H1 (Δk=0)**, **H3 (Δk=+4)** only.
- Epoch: **120 s**; **min_slice**: **60 s** (no realloc faster than epoch).
- Allocator: **UCB1** on controller-integer Δgap/sec; ignore micro-deltas (improve_eps_pct ≥ 0.2%).
- Integrator cadence: POPMUSIC merges at **T=½ wall** and **T=wall** only.
- Bee-Ball: gated; require ring score high **and** dual-gap stall ≥ **2 epochs**; strict time-box.
- Shared: champion (read-only to miners), global EHM, **portals** (top cross-zone connectors).
- Or-opt: OFF unless time_frac ≥ 0.8 (see §4).

3) Boundary & candidates (for SH and MH-Lite)
- Halo: **10–15%**. Avoid >20%.
- Cross-zone: **portals-only** (small fixed budget per node).
- Per-node edge cap: **5–10** in union/candidate graph.
- Δk profiles **must not invent far edges**; widen locally only.

4) Or-opt timing (late-only + protection)
- 0–50% wall: **FORBID** Or-opt.
- 50–80%: **ALLOW intra-zone only**, protect portal edges.
- ≥80%: **ONE global pass**, require integer gain τ ≥ 3–5; never remove protected portals unless time_frac ≥ 0.8 and τ met.

5) Logging & diagnostics (per epoch)
- Time breakdown: {local_search, merge, allocator, idle}.
- Cross-zone edge % in champion (+Δ).
- Portal removals pre-80% (count; ΔL_int at removal).
- Or-opt survival % (edges changed by Or-opt that survive to final).
- Candidate degree histogram near halos (cap violations).
- Dual-gap (HK) series; stall flags; merge events {mid,end}; allocator decisions.

6) Experiments to (re)run under 16R
A/B (primary, median over seeds 42/123/456):
- pr2392 @600 s: **SH vs MH-Lite**.
- fnl4461 @1800 s: **SH vs MH-Lite**.
Ablations (pr2392 @600 s unless noted):
- A1) SH: k_base vs k_base+4.
- A2) MH-Lite: epoch 60 vs 120 (min_slice=60).
- A3) MH-Lite: merges {each_epoch} vs {mid,end}.
- A4) MH-Lite: Bee-Ball ON vs OFF.

Capture for each: **TTT@10%** (primary), TTT@5% (if hit), final gap @ wall, early anytime slope (0–10 min), improvements/min, hive attribution, dual-gap timeline. Emit `.tour` + SHA256 + manifest JSONL.

7) Promotion gates (MH-Lite remains OFF unless all hold, medians over seeds)
- pr2392@600 s: TTT@10% ≤ SH, **or** same TTT with early slope ≥ SH and final gap ≤ SH +1%.
- fnl4461@1800 s: final gap ≤ SH +1% and early slope ≥ SH −0.5%.

8) Rollback / safety
- Any synthetic evaluator call, parity FAIL on .opt, or allocator fed non-integer deltas ⇒ **disable MH-Lite immediately** and rerun SH baselines.
