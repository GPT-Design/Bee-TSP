Update_22.txt
Title: Sunset Multi-Hive; Single-Hive (SH) as Sole Production Preset
Version: 2025-09-14
Depends on: 15, 16R, 17, 19, 20
Status: Approved

0) Decision
Multi-Hive (MH / MH-Lite) is archived as experimental. Single-Hive (SH) is the only production preset.

1) Rationale (brief)
Short-wall: MH loses or ties SH. Long-wall (pla33810): small gains (<0.2%)—below promotion gates, not worth complexity/overhead.

2) Policy
- Default mode = SH. All CI, benchmarks, and leaderboards use SH only.
- MH paths remain compilable but OFF by default and excluded from CI runs.
- Aggregator hides MH rows unless `--include-experimental` flag is set.

3) Code/config switches
- YAML defaults:

solver:
multi_hive:
enabled: false

- CLI: remove any default that auto-enables MH; require explicit `--mode MH-Lite` to run.
- Bridges: keep tour-only contract; no length reporting (already enforced).
- Output/manifest: unchanged (Update_20).

4) CI rules
- Do not schedule MH runs in CI matrices.
- `validate_update20_compliance.py` and `artifact_guard.py` remain mandatory for SH.
- `aggregate_ab_tables.py` excludes MH unless `--include-experimental`.

5) Archival note (docs)
- Mark MH/MH-Lite “Archived (Experimental)” in docs.
- Link to Update_16R results + thresholds; note failure to meet promotion gates.

6) Re-admission criteria (future)
Promote MH only if, across ≥10 seeds:
- Long-wall ≥ 3600 s shows ≥1.0–2.0% median improvement vs SH on best_len or TTT@10%, with clean guardrails and time integrity.

7) Focus areas for SH (next sprints)
- Candidate graph: edge cap 8–10; portals/node 3–7; keep halo 10–15%.
- POPMUSIC cadence: merges at mid & end only.
- Or-opt: late-only (0–50% forbid, 50–80% intra-zone, ≥80% one global pass, τ≥3–5).
- Bee-Ball: gated by HK dual-gap stall ≥2 epochs; tight time-box.
- Bounds: speed/robustness of HK 1-tree on the sparse union.
- Early-slope: seed diversification (greedy variants), keep TSPLIB-integer everywhere.

8) Aggregator tweak (optional)
Default: hide MH rows. Expose with `--include-experimental`.
